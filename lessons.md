- [2026-11-25] Wrote a bare "PR #1" for the Dependabot setup-uv PR; the app linked it to ticket-prioritization-api#1, another repository with the same number. Also said the bump was "under your one-week cooldown" when the github-actions Dependabot entry has no cooldown; the wait was a suggestion, not configuration → Always write PR and issue references as full links with owner/repo. Describe a cooldown or other control as configured only after reading the config that enforces it; otherwise say it is a manual recommendation.
